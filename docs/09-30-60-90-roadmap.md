## 2. Detailed Phase Breakdown

### Phase 1: Days 1–30 — Pilot Validation & Core Workflow Wedge
**Objective:** Onboard 5–10 pilot independent workshops in Cape Town / Johannesburg and prove that digital quoting reduces quote-to-approval turnaround from 4 hours to under 15 minutes.

- **Milestone 1.1: Core Vertical Slice Deployment**
  - Production deployment of Next.js App Router + Supabase RLS.
  - Multi-tenant workshop onboarding: Business profile, labor rate, default markup.
  - Customer directory and vehicle registry (VIN lookup, mileage).
- **Milestone 1.2: Parts & Supplier Pricing Matrix**
  - Master catalogue of top 500 fast-moving service parts (filters, brake pads, spark plugs, fluids).
  - Multi-supplier price comparison interface showing cost, lead time, and stock.
- **Milestone 1.3: Digital Customer Approval via WhatsApp**
  - Public responsive quotation approval link sent via WhatsApp / SMS.
  - One-click customer acceptance with digital signature.
  - Immediate notification back to workshop dashboard.
- **Success Metrics:**
  - $> 100$ live quotations generated.
  - Median quote turnaround time reduced by $> 50\%$.
  - 0 cross-tenant data leaks.

---

### Phase 2: Days 31–60 — Supplier Automation & Integrated Payments
**Objective:** Automate manual procurement friction and monetize transaction flow.

- **Milestone 2.1: Live Supplier Catalogue API Adapters**
  - Integrate live REST/GraphQL APIs with major automotive parts distributors (e.g. Bosch, Midas, Goldwagen).
  - Real-time inventory sync and automated wholesale price updating.
- **Milestone 2.2: Automated Purchase Order Dispatch**
  - When quote is approved, automatically convert to supplier purchase order.
  - Electronic dispatch directly into supplier warehouse management systems.
- **Milestone 2.3: Integrated Payment Gateway**
  - Embedded payment links via PayFast / Stitch / Stripe.
  - Support for instant EFT, credit card, and customer deposit payments.
  - Automated reconciliation between quote, payment, and workshop bank account.
- **Milestone 2.4: Workshop Business Intelligence**
  - Real-time dashboard for parts margin profit, technician labor efficiency, and conversion rate by vehicle make.
- **Success Metrics:**
  - $20\%+$ of approved quotations paid directly through platform.
  - Supplier order dispatch errors $< 1\%$.

---

### Phase 3: Days 61–90 — Scale, Telematics & Network Effects
**Objective:** Expand from single workshops to dealership groups and vehicle fleet integrations.

- **Milestone 3.1: Multi-Branch & Franchise Dealership Support**
  - Parent-child business hierarchy for workshop franchises and multi-location dealerships.
  - Centralized inventory pooling and inter-branch parts transfer.
- **Milestone 3.2: Mobile PWA for Workshop Floor Mechanics**
  - Mobile-optimized interface for technicians in the repair bay.
  - Voice-to-text inspection notes, photo upload of worn parts (e.g. cracked brake discs) attached directly to quotation.
- **Milestone 3.3: Connected Vehicle & Telematics Ingestion**
  - OBD-II dongle API connector for fleet customers.
  - Automated DTC (Diagnostic Trouble Code) ingestion triggering proactive quotation generation for required maintenance.
- **Success Metrics:**
  - 50+ active workshops on platform.
  - 10,000+ registered vehicles in ecosystem.
  - Platform processing $> \$250,000$ in monthly GMV.
